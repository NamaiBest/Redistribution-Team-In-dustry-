How the planner works

A shortage is a signal. A buyer makes it a shipment.

India has used this kind of redistribution before. In July 2023, the government directed tomato procurement from Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Maharashtra for distribution in centres with rising retail prices.

Read the government announcement

Read both market signals

Compare reported arrivals and wholesale prices with the previous three Augusts. Low arrivals alongside higher prices flag a market worth checking. Reporting changes can also affect arrivals.

Confirm the stock and the orders

Enter saleable tonnes, buyer orders, and shelf life at the pilot hubs. The planner reserves stock for local orders and offers any excess to hubs with unmet orders.

Compare feasible transfers

The optimizer weighs freight, modeled spoilage, and unfilled orders. It respects lane capacity and remaining shelf life. The results show the plan against a no-transfer baseline.

Data and limitations

AGMARKNET
State-level reported mandi arrivals and weighted wholesale prices, August 2022–2025. Prices are a market signal. Dispatch uses arrival-gap proxies or entered stock and orders.
MoFPI / NABCONS
Historical post-harvest loss estimates for selected crops and states. Survey percentages are displayed independently from modeled spoilage.
geoBoundaries
Simplified India ADM1 geometries, sourced from DataMeet India and the Election Commission of India, under CC BY 2.5 India.
